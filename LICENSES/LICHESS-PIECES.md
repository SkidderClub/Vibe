# Lichess chess pieces

The twelve cburnett chess pieces in `src/main/resources/assets/vibe/chess/`
are by Colin M. L. Burnett, distributed under GPL-2.0-or-later.
Vibe distributes them under the [GPL version 3](GPL-3.0.txt).

Source: [Lichess lila, pinned revision](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/60acb9d51787f60fd4504b614d83114857010ed1/public/piece/cburnett).
License declaration: [Lichess COPYING.md](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/60acb9d51787f60fd4504b614d83114857010ed1/COPYING.md).

The original SVGs are included unmodified. The matching PNGs are transparent
128-pixel rasterizations made with resvg 2.6.2. Reproduce them using
`tools/import-lichess-pieces.cjs`; no artwork is fetched at client runtime.
